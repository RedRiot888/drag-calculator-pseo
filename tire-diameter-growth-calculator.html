Formula Breakdown

Dynamic Diameter = Static Diameter × (1 + (Growth % / 100))
Rollout = Diameter × π
Finish Line RPM = (Trap Speed × Rear Gear × 336) / Dynamic Diameter

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do drag tires grow at high speed?

Centrifugal force at 150+ MPH causes bias-ply and radial slick treads to expand outward, increasing effective rollout and altering finish line RPM.

How do tire construction types affect growth?

Traditional bias-ply slicks expand significantly more (6-8%) due to flexible sidewalls, whereas stiff-sidewall slicks and radial drag tires maintain much tighter dimensional stability (2-4%).
