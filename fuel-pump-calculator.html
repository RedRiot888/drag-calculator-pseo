Trackside notes

  • Density altitude: about 3% naturally aspirated power per 1,000 feet.
  • Converter slip at the finish is often kept around 5% to 8%.
  • On boost, pull roughly 1 to 2 degrees of timing for every 3 to 4 PSI.

Why does boost change the pump and not just the injector?

A 1:1 regulator raises rail pressure with manifold pressure, so the pump outlet is base pressure plus boost. The injector differential stays near the base, which is why injector size uses base pressure and the pump uses the higher number.

Why is the shopping flow bigger than engine demand?

Voltage drop, a dirty filter, and hot fuel all steal flow. The margin, 25% unless you change it, is the flow the pump still needs to make at the working pressure.

Why does a 40 PSI catalog number shrink?

Most in-tank pumps are rated near 40 PSI and fall off toward their deadhead. The table draws a straight line between those two points. Raise the deadhead if the pump's own curve holds pressure longer.
