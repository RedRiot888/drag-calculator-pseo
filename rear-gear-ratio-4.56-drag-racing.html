Formula

Gear = (Target RPM x Tire Diameter) / (Trap MPH x Trans Final x 336). The set to order is the closest catalog ratio for that rear end. Finish RPM = (Trap MPH x Overall Ratio x 336) / Tire Diameter.

Trackside notes

4.56 Rear Gear Drag Racing Calculator. Loaded defaults: 4.56 rear gear, 32.0 inch tire.

  • Density altitude: about 3% naturally aspirated power per 1,000 feet.
  • Converter slip at the finish is often kept around 5% to 8%.
  • On boost, pull roughly 1 to 2 degrees of timing for every 3 to 4 PSI.

How does gear ratio change finish-line RPM?

A higher numeric gear raises engine RPM at the same trap speed and tire diameter.

What is a useful drag gear window?

The gear should put the engine near peak power at the stripe without forcing an extra shift. A street gear trades that RPM for cruise.

Why measure the tire at speed?

Growth makes the tire taller, which drops RPM. Size the gear with the grown diameter.

Why does the same ratio need a different gear?

Each housing has its own ring diameter and its own catalog ratios. A Ford 9-inch 4.10 is the 4.11 set. A Dana 60 4.56 also needs the 4.56-and-up carrier.
