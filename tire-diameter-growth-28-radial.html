Trackside notes

28 Inch Radial Tire Growth Calculator. Loaded defaults: 28.0 inch tire, 2.5% growth estimate.

  • Density altitude: about 3% naturally aspirated power per 1,000 feet.
  • Converter slip at the finish is often kept around 5% to 8%.
  • On boost, pull roughly 1 to 2 degrees of timing for every 3 to 4 PSI.

Why do drag tires grow?

Centrifugal force stretches the carcass, so the rolling diameter is larger than the static measurement.

How does growth change the gear?

A larger dynamic diameter lowers engine RPM at trap speed. The rollout and the gear chart should use that grown number.

What growth is typical?

A radial might grow a few percent. A slick at speed can grow more. The percentage on this page is the estimate to test, not a measured rollout.
