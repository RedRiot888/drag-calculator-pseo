  • Density altitude: about 3% naturally aspirated power per 1,000 feet.
  • Converter slip at the finish is often kept around 5% to 8%.
  • On boost, pull roughly 1 to 2 degrees of timing for every 3 to 4 PSI.

How do you match a turbo?

Match airflow in pounds per minute and the pressure ratio to a compressor map, then check the island at the boost you will actually run.

Why can the typed boost miss the power number?

Naturally aspirated flow at that RPM and volumetric efficiency sets how much boost the horsepower target needs. A short boost number runs out of air first.

What does compressor outlet temperature tell you?

Outlet temperature rises with pressure ratio and falls as compressor efficiency improves. The intercooler has to pull that heat out before the intake valve.
