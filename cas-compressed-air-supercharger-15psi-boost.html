Trackside notes

  • Density altitude: about 3% naturally aspirated power per 1,000 feet.
  • Converter slip at the finish is often kept around 5% to 8%.
  • On boost, pull roughly 1 to 2 degrees of timing for every 3 to 4 PSI.

What is a compressed-air supercharger doing?

A stored-air system raises manifold pressure from a bottle. Pressure ratio is boost plus barometer, divided by barometer.

How long does the bottle last?

Flow rate and bottle volume set the pass. A bigger shot or a longer gear uses the supply faster.

What should the plumbing handle?

The solenoid, lines, and nozzles have to pass the mass flow at the pressure you dial in. A restriction downstream of the regulator is lost boost.
