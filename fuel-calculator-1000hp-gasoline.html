Formula

How is injector size chosen?

Rated flow is flywheel horsepower times BSFC, divided by injector count, max duty, and the pressure correction. Buy the smallest common size that stays inside the duty cap.

Why does base pressure change the rating?

Flow scales with the square root of the pressure ratio versus the 43.5 PSI rating pressure. A higher base lets the same injector support more power.

What duty cycle is safe?

A cap around 80% leaves room for the injector to open, close, and stay cool. Living at 90% or more is where lean spikes show up.
