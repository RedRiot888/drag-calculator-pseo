Formula

Running amps are the pump, fan, water pump, ignition, and ECU. Alternator idle output wants 1.2 times that. Cable circular mils = 10.75 x amps x one-way feet x 2 / (voltage x 0.03). The trans brake and the nitrous sit on the battery for the hit.

Which amps does the alternator have to cover?

The loads that stay on while the engine idles in the lanes: pump, fan, water pump, ignition, ECU. The trans brake and the nitrous are a few seconds, and the battery covers that hit.

Why size the wire from voltage drop?

Circular mils equal 10.75 times the amps times the round-trip length, divided by a 3% voltage drop. A trunk battery makes the alternator wire the long one. High-strand battery cable is what carries it, not primary wire in a loom.

Where does the fuse go?

On the battery post, ahead of the panel feed. The starter cable is its own 1/0 or 2/0 and is not fused like a running load.
