250 HP Nitrous Shot Jet Calculator

Loaded defaults: 250 HP gasoline plate at 950 PSI.

Jets are starting orifices for this shot at the bottle pressure on the gauge. A 900 to 950 PSI bottle is the usual tune window. Wet fuel pressure is often about 6 PSI at the fuel solenoid. Dry EFI uses rail pressure.

Advanced / Conditions

A fogger here is two nozzles. Direct port uses one nozzle per cylinder. Leave the controller on Full hit unless a progressive box is ramping the solenoid.

BHP uses the power entry as flywheel power. WHP treats it as wheel power and converts with this drivetrain loss.

Hit horsepower is the base engine plus the full shot. Average horsepower is the button with a progressive ramp or a second-stage delay. Weigh the bottle after a timed hit before trusting the flow.

Formula

Nitrous lb/hr = Shot HP x 3.6 once the jet is sized for that shot. Jet diameter follows the shot per nozzle and the square root of bottle pressure over 900 PSI. The fuel jet feeds that nitrous at the fuel's nitrous-to-fuel ratio. Gasoline timing pull is 2 degrees per 50 HP.

Trackside notes

  • Density altitude: about 3% naturally aspirated power per 1,000 feet.
  • Converter slip at the finish is often kept around 5% to 8%.
  • On boost, pull roughly 1 to 2 degrees of timing for every 3 to 4 PSI.

Why does bottle pressure change the jet?

A nitrous jet is a liquid orifice. More bottle pressure pushes more nitrous through the same hole, so the hit gets richer on nitrous and the fuel jet is suddenly small. Hold the bottle near 900 to 950 PSI.

How much timing does a shot want?

Gasoline starts at about 2 degrees pulled for every 50 HP of nitrous. E85 and race gas want less, and methanol wants less again. The first hit on gasoline can stand another 2 degrees of reserve.

What is the difference between a wet plate and a dry shot?

A wet system brings its own fuel through a fuel jet and a fuel solenoid. A dry system sprays only nitrous, and the injectors have to add the fuel. The duty cycle is the check on a dry car.
