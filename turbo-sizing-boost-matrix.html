Adjust parameters to calculate required airflow (lbs/min) and pressure ratio.

Formula Used

Airflow (lbs/min) = (Horsepower × BSFC) / 6.0
Pressure Ratio = (Boost PSI + 14.7) / 14.7

Frequently Asked Questions

What BSFC value should I use?

Standard gasoline engines typically use 0.60, while E85 setups use ~0.85 and methanol setups use ~1.20.

How do I read a compressor map?

Match your required airflow (lbs/min) on the vertical axis and pressure ratio on the horizontal axis to see if the turbo operates in peak efficiency.
