  • Density altitude: about 3% naturally aspirated power per 1,000 feet.
  • Converter slip at the finish is often kept around 5% to 8%.
  • On boost, pull roughly 1 to 2 degrees of timing for every 3 to 4 PSI.

How are primary tubes sized?

Primary diameter follows horsepower per cylinder and the RPM where you want the torque. Bigger primaries move the peak up and can hurt the bottom.

What about a turbo downpipe?

The downpipe has to carry the turbine discharge without becoming the restriction. Area matters more than a huge open collector on a turbo car.

Why does backpressure hurt a boosted engine?

Excess backpressure raises turbine drive pressure, adds heat, and makes the same boost harder to control.
