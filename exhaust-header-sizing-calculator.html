Track-Side Tuning Cheat Sheets

Essential round-by-round reference guides for the trailer:

1. Density Altitude (DA) & Weather

Every 1,000 ft increase in DA costs naturally aspirated engines ~3% horsepower. Forced induction setups compensate better via wastegate control, but watch inlet air temps.

2. Torque Converter Slip

Target 5% to 8% slip at the finish line for max efficiency. Excessive slip generates transmission heat and kills trap speed; too tight kills 60-foot times.

3. Boost & Nitro Management

Read spark plug ground straps for heat mark placement. Back off timing 1 to 2 degrees per additional 3-4 PSI of boost or when stepping up nitrous jetting.

4. 4-Link Anti-Squat & Launch

If the car bounces or unloads the tires immediately on launch, lower the instantaneous center (IC) or soften rear shock extension.

Formula Breakdown

Frequently Asked Questions

What size header primary is best for a Naturally Aspirated drag car?

Too large of a primary tube loses exhaust velocity and scavenging at launch; too small chokes high-RPM horsepower. This calculator determines the optimal primary outer diameter based on cubic inches and peak RPM.

Why is turbo downpipe sizing critical?

In a turbo setup, the downpipe is the primary restriction after the turbine wheel. Restrictive downpipes increase backpressure, raising EGTs and robbing top-end boost efficiency.
