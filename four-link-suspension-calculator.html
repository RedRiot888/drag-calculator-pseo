What is instant center?

Instant center is where the upper and lower link lines meet. Its height versus the center of gravity sets anti-squat.

What anti-squat range do drag cars use?

Many door cars land around 100% to 140% anti-squat so the rear plants without unloading the front too early.

Why do small hole moves matter?

Moving a front or rear mount an inch changes the intersection a lot. Measure from the axle center and the ground, then change one hole at a time.
