Frequently Asked Questions

How do I increase boost?

You can increase boost by installing a smaller blower pulley or a larger crankshaft pulley to overdrive the supercharger speed.

Does belt slip affect boost?

Yes, belt slip under high load reduces effective blower RPM and causes a drop in peak boost pressure.
