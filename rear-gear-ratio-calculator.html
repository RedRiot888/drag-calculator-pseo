Frequently Asked Questions

How does changing rear gear affect finish line RPM?

Going from a 3.42 to a 4.11 gear increases your finish line RPM significantly, ensuring you cross the stripe right at your peak horsepower band.

What gear is best for quarter-mile drag racing?

The optimal rear gear matches your tire height and engine redline so that you cross the 1320-foot finish line at or slightly above peak horsepower RPM in your final 1:1 gear.
