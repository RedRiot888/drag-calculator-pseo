How is the circuit current found?

Load amps are watts divided by voltage. Size the wire, fuse, and relay above that, and apply a duty factor for a fan or pump that is not on full time.

Why size the wire for the run length?

Voltage drop grows with current and length. A fuel pump at the back of the car needs a larger gauge than the same pump next to the battery.

What does the relay protect?

The relay lets a small switch current close a high-current path. The fuse protects the wire, and it belongs at the power source.
