Frequently Asked Questions

How is shift recovery RPM calculated?

Shift recovery is determined by multiplying your shift RPM by the ratio of the upcoming higher gear divided by the current gear.

Why do manufacturer presets and custom gear sets matter?

Manufacturer presets give you instant access to factory and race-proven gear stacks across GM, Ford, Mopar, and imports, while custom ratios accommodate custom gearboxes.
