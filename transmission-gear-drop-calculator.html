Formula Breakdown

Shift Speed = (Shift RPM × Tire Diameter) / (Overall Ratio × 336)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is shift recovery RPM calculated?

Shift recovery is determined by multiplying your shift RPM by the ratio of the upcoming higher gear divided by the current gear.

Why does gear drop matter?

Keeping your engine operating inside its peak power band after a shift prevents acceleration lag down the track.
