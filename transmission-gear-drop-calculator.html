Formula

Overall Ratio = Trans Ratio x Rear Gear. Shift Speed (MPH) = (RPM x (1 - Slip) x Tire) / (Overall x 336). Recovery RPM = Shift RPM x (1 - Slip) x (Next Ratio / Current Ratio).

Trackside notes

  • Density altitude: about 3% naturally aspirated power per 1,000 feet.
  • Converter slip at the finish is often kept around 5% to 8%.
  • On boost, pull roughly 1 to 2 degrees of timing for every 3 to 4 PSI.

How is shift recovery RPM calculated?

Recovery RPM is the shift RPM times (1 minus converter slip) times the next gear divided by the gear you are leaving. At 0% slip that is the straight gear drop, and it still needs to land above converter stall.

What does the 336 constant represent?

Miles per hour equals RPM times (1 minus converter slip) times tire diameter, divided by overall ratio times 336. Overall ratio is the transmission ratio times the rear gear.

Why does a taller tire change the chart?

A taller tire lowers engine RPM at the same trap speed. Use the grown diameter, not the sidewall number, once the tire is at speed.

Why pick the rear end to buy a gear?

The ratio alone is not a part. A 3.73 Ford 9-inch set does not fit an 8.8, a 12-bolt, or a Dana 60. The housing sets the ring, the pinion, and the carrier.
