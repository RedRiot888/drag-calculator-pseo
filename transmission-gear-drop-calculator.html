Formula Breakdown

Overall Ratio = Transmission Ratio x Rear Gear Ratio
Shift Speed = (Shift RPM x Tire Diameter) / (Overall Ratio x 336)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is shift recovery RPM calculated?

Shift recovery is determined by multiplying your shift RPM by the ratio of the upcoming higher gear divided by the current gear.
